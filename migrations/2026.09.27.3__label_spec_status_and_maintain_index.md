# Label Specification Status And Maintain Its Index

Migration ID: `2026.09.27.3__label_spec_status_and_maintain_index`

## Purpose

Make current product contracts distinguishable from plans and history, and keep
new or moved specifications discoverable without expanding Git finish.

## Accepted Guidance

- Label implemented, planned, and historical behavior where they could be
  confused. Mixed documents may use clearly labeled sections.
- Support current-implementation claims with a last-check date and source or
  test paths. A date alone is not evidence; refresh status and evidence when
  relevant behavior changes, not on unrelated edits.
- When adding, renaming, moving, or retiring a project-memory specification,
  update its entry in `tools/project-memory/README.md` or the canonical spec
  index and verify the relative link in the same scoped implementation change.
  Keep the index a concise map rather than a copy of the contracts.
- Do not move these checks into `ги пуш` or another Git-finish command.
