# Enforce System Mechanism And Responsibility Gate

Migration ID: `2026.10.13.2__enforce_system_mechanism_and_responsibility_gate`

## Purpose

Require agents to correct causes at the owner of a responsibility and use or
extend existing mechanisms, instead of layering undocumented compensation over
symptoms. Keep the principle independent of a product, stack, or architecture.

## Accepted Guidance

- Before meaningful implementation, bug fixes, or refactoring, trace the
  affected scenario and distinguish working mechanisms from incomplete
  scaffolding, disabled paths, and historical notes.
- Record a scoped scenario -> mechanism -> contract owner -> change location
  -> verification mapping with current source references in the task plan or
  relevant local contract. Reuse equivalent existing context.
- Correct the violated contract at its owner. Extend an incomplete mechanism
  coherently. Justify a new mechanism or bypass with the concrete constraint,
  responsibility, scope, side effects, verification, and removal/review condition.
- Keep ownership, handoff, resources, completion, cancellation, and failure
  explicit when relevant. Repeated caller compensation indicates a shared
  primitive's contract needs review. Do not destroy valid state and restore it
  to simulate success; preserve legitimate rollback and recovery contracts.
- Verify representative scenarios, invariants, and relevant intermediate side
  effects through participating components. Tests mirroring the implementation
  and isolated checks do not prove an unverified live integration.
- Scale the gate to the change. It does not require a new approval, broad audit,
  mandatory framework, abstraction layer, or document for every small edit.
