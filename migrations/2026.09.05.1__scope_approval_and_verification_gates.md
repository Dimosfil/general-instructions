# Scope Approval And Verification Gates

Migration ID: `2026.09.05.1__scope_approval_and_verification_gates`

## Purpose

Prevent clear tasks from stopping for a goal restatement, repeated approval,
an independent blocked operation, or unnecessary repeated verification.

## Accepted Guidance

- Reuse session authorization for the same action, target, and scope; reassess
  material changes and preserve explicit safety and approval requirements.
- Derive observable success criteria from a clear bounded request without a
  separate project-goal interview. Clarify material unknowns before dependent work.
- Stop only the blocked operation and dependent steps; finish independent
  authorized work and report incomplete requirements honestly.
- Identify the exact instruction and its applicability when a rule causes a
  pause; do not infer approval gates from general recommendations.
- Complete sufficient and mandatory checks, then expand or repeat only for new
  changes, failures, unresolved concerns, or required gates. Explicit full-test
  and release workflows remain mandatory.
