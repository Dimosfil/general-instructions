# Verify Document Conclusions With Quotes

Version: `2026.10.18.2`

## Accepted Contract

Extend the existing optional final task-result review for document-based work.
After execution, before the final response, reread the source and delivered text,
revisit disputed passages with context, and test assumptions and alternative
interpretations. For every conclusion drawn from a document, identify its
point/page/section and give a short verbatim supporting quotation next to the
conclusion in the delivered analysis/document. Distinguish source wording from
inference. Do not fabricate numbering, quotes, or confirmation; state evidence,
OCR, access, and quotation-limit gaps and conflicting passages explicitly.
